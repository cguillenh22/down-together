**Quick answer.** Hypotonia (low muscle tone) is common in babies and children with Down syndrome. Regular exercises at home strengthen muscles naturally.

**Why it matters.** Better muscle tone improves posture, balance, movement ability, and independence in daily activities.

**What the source says.** Physical therapists recommend active play, supervised floor time, frequent position changes, and activities that gradually challenge the child.

**What can you do now?** Integrate exercises into daily play: encourage crawling, stair climbing, ball play, gentle races. Fun is key to adherence.

**To go deeper.** Changes take time. Follow up with your physical therapist monthly to adjust exercises and measure progress.
