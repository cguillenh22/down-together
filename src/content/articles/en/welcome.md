This space exists because of a real gap: when a Down syndrome diagnosis arrives, clear, current information in your own language isn't always easy to find. Here we gather, in both Spanish and English, content based on verified sources — recognized Down syndrome associations and health organizations — organized into two levels:

- **The basics**: frequently asked questions and fundamentals for those new to this journey.
- **News & research**: updates and studies for readers who already know the essentials.

Every article is drafted with Claude's assistance from trusted sources, and reviewed by a human before it's published.
